:tada: Our work on **ICP for Data Attribution** was accepted to **NAACL 2025**. We showed that simple probing of LLMs may serve as a practical proxy for gradient-based data attribution, enabling efficient identification of influential training samples. Check out our [:page_facing_up: paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.12259) and [:computer: code](https://github.com/cxcscmu/InContextDataAttribution).
